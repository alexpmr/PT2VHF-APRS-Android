## English (U.S.)

**Name:** PT2VHF APRS Client

**Subtitle:** APRS for ham radio operators

**Promotional text:**
See APRS stations on the map, exchange acknowledged messages and send your position over the internet or by radio
with a Bluetooth LE TNC. Free and ad-free.

**Keywords:**
aprs,ham radio,amateur radio,tnc,kiss,aprs-is,tracker,vhf,uhf,mic-e,beacon,bluetooth,callsign

**Description:**

PT2VHF APRS Client is an APRS client for radio amateurs, free and ad-free. It is the same app as on Android, now
on iPhone and iPad, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and Italian.

LIVE MAP
• APRS stations, objects and items on Apple Maps, with callsign and official symbol
• Map styles: Standard, Dark (great at night and in the car), Satellite, Hybrid and Automatic
• Station details: distance, bearing, speed, path (digipeaters) and raw packet

MESSAGES
• Conversations by callsign, with delivery acknowledgement (ACK) and up to 5 automatic retries
• "Give up ACK and resend" in one tap
• Bulletins (BLN) and general messages (CQ) in their own conversations
• Notifications by type: personal, bulletins, general and delivery (ACK)
• Block a callsign

POSITION (BEACON)
• "Beacon now" and automatic tracker by interval and on turns
• Keeps sending in the background if you allow "Always" location access

INTERNET OR RADIO
• APRS-IS with automatic reconnect and a range filter around you
• KISS TNC over Bluetooth LE (Mobilinkd TNC3/TNC4, PicoAPRS, B.B. Link and radios with KISS BLE)
• List of received and sent packets, Mic-E decoding
• APRS-IS passcode computed from your callsign, no sign-up and no account

Without a callsign the app is receive-only: it shows stations and messages but never transmits. Transmitting
requires an amateur radio licence. Classic Bluetooth (SPP) TNCs do not work on iPhone due to an iOS limitation;
use a Bluetooth LE TNC.

More information: www.pt2vhf.com

**What's new (1.4):**
First release for iPhone and iPad, with the features of PT2VHF APRS Client 1.4 for Android: reworked message ACK,
give up ACK and resend, notifications by type, dark map and Bluetooth LE TNC.

## Privacidade do app (App Store Connect → Privacidade do app)

Mesmas respostas da "Segurança dos dados" do Google Play e do `ios/PT2VHFAPRS/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`.

- **Coleta dados?** Sim.
- **Localização → Localização precisa:** usada para *Funcionalidade do app*; **vinculada** à identidade (vai junto
  com o indicativo); **não** usada para rastreamento. Sai do aparelho no beacon (que o usuário liga) e, de forma
  aproximada, no filtro de raio enviado ao servidor APRS-IS.
- **Identificadores → ID do usuário:** o indicativo de radioamador. *Funcionalidade do app*, vinculado, sem
  rastreamento.
- **Conteúdo do usuário → Outro conteúdo do usuário:** mensagens APRS enviadas. *Funcionalidade do app*,
  vinculado, sem rastreamento.
- **Não coleta:** contato, saúde, finanças, contatos, histórico de navegação/pesquisa, compras, diagnóstico, dados
  de uso, anúncios. Não há conta, analytics nem SDK de terceiros.
- Observação: a rede APRS é pública por natureza; o que o usuário transmite fica visível para qualquer estação e
  sites como aprs.fi. O app iOS não envia nada para pt2vhf.com.

## Conformidade de exportação

O app não usa criptografia própria nem TLS (APRS-IS é TCP simples). `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` já
está no Info.plist, então o App Store Connect não pergunta nada.

## Notas para a revisão da Apple (App Review Information → Notes)

Sem login: deixar "Sign-in required" desmarcado. Contato: nome, telefone e e-mail do mantenedor (preencher no
App Store Connect; não vão para a ficha pública).

```
PT2VHF APRS Client is an APRS (Automatic Packet Reporting System) client for licensed amateur radio operators.
APRS is a public, open network where radio amateurs share positions and short text messages, over radio and
over the internet (APRS-IS servers such as rotate.aprs2.net).

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT A LICENCE
1. Open the app. On the welcome screen leave the callsign empty and tap "Done".
2. The app connects to APRS-IS in receive-only mode (login N0CALL, passcode -1). Allow location so the map
   centers on you: stations near Cupertino appear on the map and in the "Stations" tab within a
   minute. Nothing is transmitted in this mode; the server rejects any transmission.
3. "Messages" shows bulletins and messages addressed to the user's callsign. Callsigns can be blocked (swipe
   the conversation or long-press a message; list in Settings > Blocked).
Transmitting (beacon, messages) requires entering a valid amateur radio callsign, which only licensed operators
have; the APRS-IS passcode is derived from the callsign, as in every APRS client.

PERMISSIONS
- Location "When In Use": show the user's position and nearby stations, and send the APRS position beacon.
- Location "Always" + background mode "location": only requested when the user turns on the tracker
  (Settings > Beacon > "Tracker on"), so the position keeps being beaconed while driving with the screen
  off. This is the core use of APRS trackers.
- Bluetooth + background mode "bluetooth-central": connection to a Bluetooth LE KISS TNC (radio modem). Hardware
  is not needed to review; without it the app works over the internet.

The app follows the device language (English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian). No account, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases.
```
