## English

**Name:** PT2VHF APRS Client

**Short description:** APRS client for ham radio: map, messages, tracker, TNC and APRS-IS.

**Full description:**

PT2VHF APRS Client is a full APRS client for licensed radio amateurs, free and ad-free.

• Live map with stations, objects, tracks and map styles (standard, dark, grey, topographic)
• APRS messaging with acknowledgements (ACK), grouped conversations, bulletins and resend
• Tracker with SmartBeaconing and usage profiles
• Connect via the internet (APRS-IS) or via radio: Bluetooth TNC (classic and BLE), USB and audio TNC (AFSK)
• Works with radios such as Kenwood TH-D74/TH-D75, ICOM ID-5200 and KISS TNCs
• iGate and smart digipeater on the phone
• Packet decoder, full Mic-E and device identification for each station
• Android Auto, home screen widget and car mode
• In Portuguese, English, Spanish, French and Italian

A ham radio licence is required to transmit. iOS and other platforms coming soon.
More information: www.pt2vhf.com
